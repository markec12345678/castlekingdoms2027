## Game Title
Stronghold 2027

## Short Description (312 chars max)
Build, defend, and conquer in this medieval castle RTS. Dynamic economy, adaptive AI opponents, and a 10-mission campaign await. Forge your kingdom from ruin to throne.

## Detailed Description

Sir Markus returns from exile to reclaim his birthright. Rebuild the ruined castle of Fernhaven, forge alliances, and march on the capital to claim the throne of Valdemar.

**BUILD YOUR KINGDOM**
- 71 unique buildings: farms, workshops, barracks, cathedrals
- Dynamic economy with supply/demand pricing across 20 resources
- 4 seasons that affect production and prices
- 10 random economic events (blight, gold rush, plague, festival)
- Trade caravans to AI factions with escort and risk system

**COMMAND YOUR ARMY**
- 8 military unit types (Archers, Knights, Crossbowmen, and more)
- Real-time combat with screen shake, hit flash, and punch zoom
- Siege warfare with catapults and trebuchets
- Combat order visualizer (see attack lines and move orders)
- Formation system (line, column, wedge, spread)

**FACE INTELLIGENT OPPONENTS**
- 4 AI personalities: Aggressive, Balanced, Defensive, Economic
- 4 difficulty levels: Easy, Medium, Hard, Brutal
- AI adapts to your playstyle and defends its keep
- Smart building placement (no clustering)
- Strategic attack timing (not random)

**EXPERIENCE THE CAMPAIGN**
- 10-mission story campaign "The Lord of Fernhaven"
- Progressive difficulty from tutorial to final siege
- 6-phase final battle (capture village → breach walls → final assault)
- Meet Sir Markus, Lady Elara, Lord Draven, and Brother Cedric
- Campaign progress saved between sessions
- 3 achievements to unlock

**IMMERSIVE WORLD**
- Day/night cycle with dynamic lighting
- 6 weather types (clear, rain, heavy rain, snow, fog, storm)
- HD shaders (bloom, color grading, vignette)
- Torch and fire light sources with flicker
- Ambient sound crossfading (wind, birds, fire, rain)
- 16 languages supported

**MODDING SUPPORT**
- Built-in modding API (alpha)
- Lifecycle hooks (onLoad, onTick, onBuildingPlaced)
- Example mod included
- Sandboxed environment for safety

**PERFORMANCE**
- Built-in performance profiler (F3)
- Tiered entity updates (60Hz/10Hz/2Hz)
- AI tick optimizer (5 frequency categories)
- Memory leak detection
- Settings persistence (settings.json)
- Auto-save every 5 minutes
