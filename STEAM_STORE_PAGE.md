## About This Game

Castle Kingdoms 2027 is a modernized reimagining of the classic 2001 castle-building RTS, built for a new generation of strategy players. Construct mighty fortresses, manage a thriving medieval economy, and lead your armies to victory in an immersive isometric world.

## Key Features

### Build Your Kingdom
- Construct 50+ unique buildings across multiple upgrade tiers
- Manage a dynamic economy with supply/demand, inflation, and seasonal modifiers
- Gather 10 different resources: wood, stone, iron, gold, food, pitch, ale, bows, swords, spears
- Build advanced production chains: farms → mills → bakeries → food
- **NEW: 990 Royal Maker production systems** with full Tech Tree (891 dependencies, 165 chains)
- **NEW: Dynamic Market Dashboard** with real-time prices, trends, and event tracking

### Wage War
- Command 9 unit types from humble Archers to mighty Knights
- 5 battle formations (line, column, wedge, scatter, box) with unique bonuses
- Units gain experience through combat — 5 veterancy levels from Novice to Legendary
- Deploy 4 siege weapons: catapults, trebuchets, siege towers, and battering rams
- Dynamic weather affects combat: rain boosts farms, fog reduces vision, snow slows units
- **NEW: Combat Morale System** — units flee when losing, rally with Lord abilities, formation bonuses
- **NEW: Procedural Combat SFX** — 12 battle sounds generated from waveforms (no audio files needed)
- **NEW: Anti-Clustering System** — soft repulsion forces prevent unit overlap in combat
- **NEW: Performance LOD System** — auto-adjusts detail for 100+ units (50% update reduction)

### Play Your Way
- **Campaign**: 10 story-driven missions with Slovenian voice-over and cutscenes
- **Skirmish Trail**: 10 progressively challenging battles against AI opponents
- **Freebuild**: Relaxed sandbox mode for creative castle building
- **Multiplayer**: Up to 8 players via TCP/IP with diplomacy, trade, and chat
- **Co-op**: Team up with a friend for cooperative campaign missions
- **Map Editor**: Create and share custom maps with the community
- **NEW: 5 Difficulty Levels** (Peaceful/Easy/Normal/Hard/Brutal) with 11 gameplay modifiers
- **NEW: 6 Color Themes** — gold, blue, green, red, dark, violet (persistent)
- **NEW: Command Palette** (Ctrl+Space) — quick search for all game functions
- **NEW: Event Log Panel** (Ctrl+Shift+L) — central combat/economy/system event diary

### Advanced AI
- 4 distinct AI personalities (Aggressive, Balanced, Defensive, Economic)
- 6 difficulty levels from Story to Nightmare
- Adaptive AI that evaluates your threat level and adjusts strategy
- AI opponents send personality-driven taunts and greetings
- **NEW: AI Morale Awareness** — AI retreats when broken, pursues fleeing enemies, adapts to morale state

### Modern Features
- HD render pipeline with normal mapping, SSAO, and ACES tone mapping
- Dynamic day/night cycle with realistic lighting
- 32 language support with runtime switching
- Full gamepad/controller support with virtual cursor
- Colorblind modes, font scaling, and reduced motion options
- Steam Workshop integration for mods
- 10 Steam achievements
- Replay system for recording and playback
- Statistics dashboard tracking lifetime stats
- **NEW: Performance & Combat Dashboard** — real-time FPS, LOD, Morale, Spacing, SFX stats
- **NEW: 14+ Modern UI Panels** — Tech Tree graph, Market Dashboard, Achievement Gallery, Statistics, Tutorial Manager, Keyboard Shortcut Editor, Unified Settings, Help Overlay, Minimap HUD, and more
- **NEW: Procedural SFX** — 12 combat sounds synthesized from mathematical waveforms
